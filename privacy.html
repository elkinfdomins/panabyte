Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

In short: panaBYTE is used by one person, its owner, to upload the owner's own original videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It reads the basic profile of the connected account and does not collect or store information about anyone else.

About panaBYTE

panaBYTE is a personal tool that runs on its owner's computer. It creates short videos about technology and AI and uploads them to the owner's TikTok account through TikTok's official developer tools. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Information the tool accesses

When the owner connects a TikTok account with Login Kit, the tool asks for the two permissions below and nothing more.

PermissionWhat it is used for
user.info.basicReads the display name and avatar of the account that authorizes the tool, so the owner can see which account is connected.
video.uploadSends a video file created by the owner to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft. The owner reviews it in the TikTok app and publishes it personally.

How information is stored

The authorization token that TikTok issues is kept only on the owner's own computer. panaBYTE does not run a server or a database, and it does not send the token anywhere except to TikTok when the owner asks for an upload.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share information with third parties. The only service that receives anything is TikTok itself, and only the video and caption the owner chose to upload.

Other people's information

The tool does not collect, process or store personal information about any other TikTok user, including followers, commenters or viewers.

Your choices

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from the app permissions section of the TikTok account settings. The locally stored token can be removed by deleting the tool's local data from the computer.

Children

The tool is not directed to children and is not offered to the public.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent by direct message to the panaBYTE account on TikTok.
