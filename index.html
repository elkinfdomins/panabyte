panaBYTE

Short tech and AI videos, uploaded to TikTok as drafts

panaBYTE is a personal desktop tool. It creates short vertical videos about technology and artificial intelligence and uploads them to its owner's TikTok account as drafts. The owner reviews every draft in the TikTok app before publishing it.

How it works

A video is rendered on the owner's computer. The owner connects their TikTok account with Login Kit, and the tool sends the finished video to the TikTok inbox through the Content Posting API. The owner then opens the TikTok app, checks the draft and decides whether to publish it.

TikTok permissions used

The tool asks only for user.info.basic and video.upload. The Privacy Policy explains what each one is used for.
